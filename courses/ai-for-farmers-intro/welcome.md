# Introduction to AI for Farmers

AI chat tools like ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini and Claude can take a lot of the office work off a farm: the roster, the neighbour notice, the contractor email, the summary of a long document. They can also make things up with total confidence. This course shows you how to get the first without getting caught by the second.

## What this course covers

Over four short modules you'll use AI on real farm jobs, see where it goes wrong, and build the habit of checking its work before anyone relies on it. You finish by setting up a small helper that answers questions from your own farm documents.

## Course structure

| Module | Title | Est. time |
| ------ | ----- | --------- |
| 1 | **AI on the farm**: what these tools are good at, how to give them a clear job, and how to check the answer | 20-25 min |
| 2 | **Farm paperwork made easier**: rosters, staff messages, neighbour spray notices, contractor emails and records | 20-25 min |
| 3 | **Making sense of farm numbers**: feed and rain records, kill sheets, app exports, and who gets to see your data | 20-25 min |
| 4 | **Build your own farm helper**: load your documents, write the rules, test it, and keep it current | 20-25 min |

**Estimated total: about 1.5 hours**

## How you'll learn

Each module has:

- **A short video** to open the topic
- **Three phone-sized lessons** with worked examples you can copy into your own AI tool
- **A hands-on activity** that runs in your browser. No AI account needed, and nothing is sent anywhere
- **A quiz** at the end. You need 80% to pass, and you can retake it

The examples use Ridgeback Farm, a made-up farm, so you can practise without sharing anything real.

## Who this course is for

Farmers, farm managers and farm staff in New Zealand who've heard about AI and want to know what it's actually useful for. You don't need any technical background, and you don't need to pay for anything to finish the course.

> **Tip:** Do the lessons and activities when you're stopped, not while driving or working machinery. Module 4 ends with a bonus podcast episode you can listen to in the ute.

This course is independent training and isn't endorsed by any of the companies or tools it mentions.
