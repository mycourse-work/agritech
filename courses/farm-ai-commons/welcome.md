# Farm AI Commons

Learn AI by building it, on your own farm. Every module ends with you making something that works, using public data about your own place: rain, rivers, soils, land class and more. You start by putting your farm on the map, and every module after that builds on it.

The course was shaped by what farmers in Kurow told us in October. Most wanted to build simple tools, not watch talks about AI. Many weren't sure they could trust the answers. So every module ends with a build, and checking the answer comes early.

## What this course covers

The course runs as 12 modules in four levels, from "what is AI" through to building your own agents and apps.

| Level | Start here if you can already | Modules |
| ----- | ----------------------------- | ------- |
| 1 Getting started | Nothing yet. You've never used AI, or tried it once or twice | 1 What AI is, and what it isn't. 2 Your data, your rules, and checking the answer. 3 Asking questions that get good answers |
| 2 Everyday AI | Use an AI tool most weeks and check what it says | 4 Your feed budget. 5 Admin, email and research. 6 Weather, climate and El Niño |
| 3 Your data | Use AI on your own spreadsheets and documents | 7 Asking your own documents. 8 Your farm on one page. 9 Connecting your apps and data |
| 4 Building | Load files into AI and want it to do jobs for you | 10 Skills and workflows. 11 Agents that do the work. 12 Build your own farm app |

**Module 1 is ready now.** The other modules follow.

## How you'll learn

Each module has:

- **Check yourself:** three quick questions at the start. Get all three right and you can go straight to the build
- **Short lessons** you can read on your phone in 5 to 10 minutes, each with a 2 to 4 minute video
- **A build:** a step-by-step job using public data about your own farm
- **Show and tell:** share what you made with your district group or at a build night
- **A quiz** at the end. You need 80% to pass, and you can retake it

## Which AI tool to use

Level 1 works in any mainstream AI chat tool, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Copilot, or Amazon Quick if your work or business has it. Use the one you already have. The course teaches skills that carry across all of them.

## Who this course is for

Farmers, farm managers and farm staff in New Zealand. You don't need any technical background to start at Level 1.

> **Tip:** Do the lessons and builds when you're stopped, not while driving or working machinery. The videos have captions, so you can watch with the sound off.

This course is independent training and isn't endorsed by any of the companies or tools it mentions. The data in the builds comes from public sources, and each one is named where it's used.
