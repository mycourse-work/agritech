# What AI can do for a farm

![AI on the farm: generic farmer and teaching materials.](/api/content/ai-for-farmers-intro/@modules/ai-for-farmers-intro-ai-on-the-farm/assets/ai-for-farmers-intro-ai-on-the-farm-hero.jpg)

Allow 3 minutes. All Example Station details are invented.

An AI chat helper produces an answer from your request and the information available to it. It can draft a short message, organise notes or explain a table. It can also add a convincing detail that nobody supplied. A fluent answer can still be wrong.

At fictional Example Station, a gate hinge needs inspection. Tuesday has been requested, but no visit is booked. AI can turn that note into an enquiry. The farmer checks that the enquiry still asks about availability.

Start with a task whose result you can check in a few minutes. Rewording a message is a useful trial. A decision about animal health, spraying or legal compliance needs the responsible person and the relevant source evidence.

You do not need an AI account for this course. Each activity runs a local simulation with invented material. You can also work through the notes on paper. The activities do not contact a real AI service.

## Try one small job

Write the job in a sentence: "Turn this gate note into a contractor enquiry." Keep the original note beside the answer. Decide who will check it before use.

If you use voice input later, practise while parked and check the transcript. Noise can change a date or number. Avoid recording other people's conversations.
