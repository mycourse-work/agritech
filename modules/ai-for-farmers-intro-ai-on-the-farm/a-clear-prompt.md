# Give the helper a clear job

Allow 3 minutes to read, then try the short activity. All Example Station details are invented.

A prompt is the instruction you type or speak to an AI helper. Give it enough information to do one job.

Example Station is fictional. Its note says: "Fence check on Tuesday. Contractor availability unknown. Gate hinge needs inspection. We have not agreed a booking."

Copy this prompt, or write the answer yourself:

```text
Draft a short contractor enquiry for fictional Example Station in NZ.
Use these facts: fence check requested for Tuesday; gate hinge needs
inspection; contractor availability is unknown; no booking is agreed.
Ask whether Tuesday suits. Keep it under 80 words. Do not add a price,
arrival time or promise. Put missing information in a separate list.
```

Read the reply beside the note. Does "Can you confirm whether Tuesday suits?" become "See you Tuesday morning"? That change turns an enquiry into a commitment.

If you dictate the prompt, review the transcript before submitting it. Background noise can change a date or a number. Try voice practice when parked. Avoid recording other people's conversations.

On a phone, ask for short paragraphs or a small checklist. You can follow up with "Shorten this and keep the uncertainty about Tuesday."
