# Check yourself

Three quick questions before you start. They take about two minutes, and nothing you pick is saved or sent anywhere.

> **Placement:** If you get all three right, you already know the ground this module covers. You can jump straight to the build, where you ask AI about your own farm.

## Got all 3?

Jump to the build. Open the lesson list and tap **Build: Ask about your farm**. You can come back to the lessons any time, and you'll still need the module quiz at the end.

## Missed one or more?

That's what the lessons are for. Tap **Next** to start with **AI in plain English**, and work through in order. Each lesson takes 5 to 10 minutes on your phone, and the build is waiting at the end.
