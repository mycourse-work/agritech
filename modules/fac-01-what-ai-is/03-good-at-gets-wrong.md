# What it's good at, and what it gets wrong

A chat AI can turn a page of rough notes into a tidy email in ten seconds. It can also tell you, just as smoothly, about a rule that doesn't exist.

> **On the farm:** Most of the time AI is useful. The risk is that a wrong answer sounds exactly like a right one, so you can't tell them apart by how sure it sounds.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

- Name four jobs chat AI does well.
- Name three ways it gets things wrong.
- Spot a made-up fact in an AI answer by checking it against a source.

## What it's good at

![A farmer in his shed looks sceptically at a chat answer on his phone, his ruled notebook in the other hand and a rain gauge on the bench.](./assets/fac-l3-gets-wrong.webp)

Chat AI does best when you give it the facts and ask it to work with them.

- **Drafting.** A text to the contractor, a notice for the neighbours, a job ad for a shepherd. You give it the details, it gives you a first draft.
- **Summarising.** Paste in a long email or a document and ask for the three things you need to do and by when.
- **Explaining.** "What does this clause in my grazing contract mean, in plain words?" or "What's a soil moisture deficit?"
- **Working through options.** "I've got more hoggets than I planned for. Give me four options and the trade-offs." It won't decide for you, but it's good at laying out choices you hadn't lined up yet.

## What it gets wrong

### 1. Made-up facts, stated with confidence

Remember how it works: it picks likely words. When it doesn't know a date, a figure or a rule, it can still write one that sounds right. People call these hallucinations. They're hardest to catch when they sit in the middle of an answer that's otherwise good.

### 2. Out-of-date knowledge

A chat AI learned from text gathered up to a point in time, so prices, rules and dates may be out of date. Some apps search the web for newer information, but not every app does, and not on every question. If it matters, ask where the figure came from and check it at the source.

### 3. Anything about your farm you haven't told it

It has never walked your paddocks. It doesn't know your stock numbers, your soils or what rain fell on your place last month. Ask it about your farm with nothing attached and it fills the gaps with what's typical for "a farm like yours", which may be nothing like yours. ## How sure is it? You can't tell from the tone

A chat AI sounds just as sure when it's guessing as when it's right. So judge the answer by what you can check, not by how it sounds:

- Does it give a source you can open?
- Do the numbers match your own records?
- If you ask the same question a different way, do you get the same answer?

Module 2 turns this into a habit called "check three things".

## Key takeaways

- Chat AI is good at drafting, summarising, explaining and laying out options.
- It can invent facts, give out-of-date information and guess about your farm.
- A confident tone tells you nothing about whether it's right.
- Give it your facts and check what it gives back.
