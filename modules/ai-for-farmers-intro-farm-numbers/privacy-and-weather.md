# Check sharing rights and weather evidence

Allow 3 minutes. All Example Station details are invented.

Before uploading a real export, check who may use it and for what purpose. Removing a name may leave a person or property identifiable through dates or linked details. Use invented documents for public demonstrations.

Ask the person responsible for privacy and the app provider about access, storage, retention, model training, deletion and export rights. Entered records and derived summaries may have different contractual rights. Paying for an app does not settle ownership or permission to reuse its data. Keep passwords and login information out of AI chats.

## Read a weather output as evidence

The drift activity is a stylised learning model, not a field prediction. Change wind speed and direction, droplet category and boom height. Observe how the pictured plume changes. The comparison starts at an illustrative 15 km/h and can be changed for teaching. It is not a legal limit or a recommended setting.

Check your regional plan, product label and GROWSAFE guidance for actual work. Wind alone cannot establish suitability. Gusts, inversion conditions, terrain, sensitive areas, equipment and job details need the operator's review. No chemical rates or operating instructions are supplied here.

The lab always retains these unresolved checks. Moving the pictured plume away from a boundary does not approve a job. Forecast weather also needs current local observations and responsible interpretation before a decision.

Use the same habit across the module: keep the source, distinguish a measurement from a model, and make gaps visible before using the answer.
