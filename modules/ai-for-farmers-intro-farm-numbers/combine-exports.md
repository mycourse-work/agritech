# Bring different app exports together carefully

Allow 3 minutes. All Example Station details are invented.

Several files can answer one question when their records describe the same thing. Begin with an index of source, export date, period, location definition, units and missing fields. Keep the original files and use working copies.

These invented weather rows belong to one practice period:

| Event | Location label | Observed rain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Upper Flat | 10 mm |
| B | upper flat | 20 mm |
| B | upper flat | 20 mm |
| C | Upper Flats | Unknown |

The repeated B row might be a duplicate download. Check the event identity and origin before removing it from a working copy. If confirmed, A and B total 30 mm. C stays unknown.

Ask AI to propose a label-matching table while preserving the originals. "Upper Flat" and "upper flat" may be formatting variants. "Upper Flats" needs confirmation; a similar name does not prove a match.

## Do not invent the connection

Check ambiguous dates such as 03/04 against the exporting app's date convention. Align daily and monthly periods, millimetres and other units, and current snapshots and history before comparison.

A sensor series of 10, 80 and 10 degrees Celsius needs an investigation. Keep the unusual reading, check the sensor, units and timing, and flag it. Do not silently delete it or call it a trend.

Ask for uncertain matches and missing readings as a separate list. If a helper has no approved connection to an app, it cannot fetch its latest data. Use an authorised export and record when it was made.
