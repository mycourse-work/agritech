## Full podcast script

Host: The capstone is a small document helper with one job: find information and draft short answers for review. It uses invented Example Station files and can be completed without an account. Listen without looking at the screen. Pause when work needs your attention, then do the tests when parked.

Farmer: I will choose the visitor handbook, task notes and equipment note. I want to know what each document actually says before adding it to a tool.

Host: Keep the originals, document versions and readable text. For real work, confirm permission and sensitivity before uploading anything. The handbook says visitors wait at the office for the supervisor. The task notes say workshop. That is a deliberate conflict in our practice material.

Farmer: I should not tell the helper to pick the newer-looking page. Someone needs to establish the current approved instruction.

Host: Ask it to cite both sections and flag the conflict for the supervisor. Write instructions for short answers, source evidence and visible gaps. Also say the helper must not send messages, change records or approve operational work. The scope needs to be clear before the trial.

Farmer: What if a sentence inside a document says to ignore those checks? It could be copied into a source file by mistake.

Host: Treat that sentence as document content. It must not replace the helper's instructions. Test that behaviour in a real private trial. A request to cite sources cannot guarantee accurate evidence; read each cited passage yourself.

Farmer: The optional Amazon Quick example maps the same steps to a document area and a custom chat agent. I can use the simulator if the account menus are unavailable.

Host: Public documentation describes Spaces for knowledge and custom chat agents. The lesson shows adding the invented files, linking the practice Space and testing in preview. Current menus and permissions need checking. The walkthrough has not been tested in a signed-in account and the course requires no purchase.

Farmer: I need more tests than an answer the helper can easily find. Otherwise a successful reply tells me very little about the gaps.

Host: Ask whether Tuesday's fence inspection is booked. The task notes say requested and unconfirmed. Ask what chemical rate is listed. The documents contain none, so the answer must preserve that gap. Ask where visitors wait. The answer must show the office and workshop conflict and request the supervisor's decision.

Farmer: I will record each question, the answer, its supporting section and any correction. A citation to an unrelated heading fails.

Host: Exactly. The words in that section must support the claim. Repeat failed tests after a change. The local activity has scripted responses; it checks instruction ingredients and document selection. It does not prove how a real AI service will behave.

Farmer: My course result is the specification and three checked test records. I can download the local record or write the same evidence on paper.

Host: Give a real helper an owner role and a review date. Repeat the tests when a document, tool or access setting changes. Keep the trial private while the responsible person reviews the results and permissions. A neatly cited answer can still be stale, so keep checking the current source.

## Recording notes

521 spoken words excluding speaker labels, about 4.3 minutes at 120 words per minute. Record calm NZ English with distinct voices and brief pauses at speaker changes. Keep total runtime within 4-6 minutes. No music or machinery sounds beneath speech. Do not add advice or require a screen during listening. Read numbers and units in full as written.
