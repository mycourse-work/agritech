# Ridgeback Farm: handbook for new staff

Updated 3 March 2026 by Hannah Cole. A printed copy lives in the dairy office.

Welcome to Ridgeback. Read this in your first week and ask Jack about anything that doesn't make sense.

## Who's who

- **Hannah Cole**, owner-manager. Pays wages, books the vet and contractors.
- **Jack Reid**, 2IC. Runs the daily jobs and the roster, does the NAIT records, looks after the machinery.
- **Priya Nair**, farm assistant. Leads calf rearing.
- **Tom Walsh**, relief milker on weekends.

Phone numbers are on the whiteboard in the dairy office.

## Your first day

- Jack does your induction: farm map, hazard register and the quad and side-by-side.
- Read the hazard register on the dairy office wall and sign it.
- You don't ride the quad or drive the side-by-side until Jack signs you off. Helmet on every time you're on the quad. Seatbelt on in the side-by-side.

## Hours and roster

- Cups on at 5:00 am and 2:30 pm.
- The roster is on the dairy office whiteboard. Ask Jack at least 48 hours ahead if you want to swap a shift.

## Visitors and contractors

Anyone who isn't staff signs the visitor book in the dairy office and waits there until Hannah or Jack meets them. Show them the hazard board by the door before they go anywhere on the farm.

## Animal health

- Don't treat a cow without checking with Hannah or Jack first.
- Every treated cow gets red tape on both back legs and goes in the treatment book in the vat room. The book shows the product, the dose and the date her milk can go back in the vat.
- Milk from a red-taped cow never goes in the vat. If you're not sure, ask before you put the cups on.
- Jack records births, deaths and movements in NAIT. Tell him the same day if a tagged animal dies or leaves the farm.

## Accidents and emergencies

- In an emergency, call 111 first, then Hannah.
- Meet at the front gate by the tanker track if we need to get everyone together.
- Tell Jack about every injury or near miss the same day. He writes it in the accident register.
- First aid kits are in the dairy office and behind the seat of the side-by-side.

## Phones and privacy

- Ask Hannah before you post photos of the farm, the stock or other staff online.
- Staff contact details stay in the office. Don't pass them on.
