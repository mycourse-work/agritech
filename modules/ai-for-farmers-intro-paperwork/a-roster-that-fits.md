# A roster that fits the note

![Farm paperwork made easier: generic farmer and teaching materials.](/api/content/ai-for-farmers-intro/@modules/ai-for-farmers-intro-paperwork/assets/ai-for-farmers-intro-paperwork-hero.jpg)

Allow 3 minutes to read, then try the short activity. All Example Station details are invented.

All inputs are made up for Example Station. Worker A is available Monday and Tuesday. Worker B is available Tuesday and Wednesday. Each day needs one morning check. Worker A cannot work Wednesday. These are practice constraints, not employment rules.

```text
Make a three-day morning-check roster using these fictional facts.
Worker A: Monday and Tuesday available; Wednesday unavailable.
Worker B: Tuesday and Wednesday available.
One worker needed each morning. Use a day/worker table.
List any gap. Do not invent availability or change the constraints.
```

A valid draft assigns A on Monday, either A or B on Tuesday, and B on Wednesday. Count the covered days yourself. A draft assigning A on Wednesday fails even if it looks tidy.

Before using a real roster, the manager checks agreed hours, breaks, leave, competence and coverage with the team. AI cannot resolve staffing gaps by quietly adding hours or assuming someone is available.

## Change the situation

Suppose B becomes unavailable Wednesday. Ask the helper to show the gap and draft a question for the manager. It should leave Wednesday unassigned. Do not paste staff health information or personal leave reasons into a practice chat.

## Reflection and reveal

Use fictional details in your response. You can write on paper if preferred.
