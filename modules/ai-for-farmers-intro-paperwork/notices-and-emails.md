# Write a notice or an enquiry

Allow 3 minutes. All Example Station details are invented.

A useful draft keeps proposals separate from commitments. Fictional Example Station is considering work in Practice Block on Friday. The exact date, time, affected neighbours and notice requirements are unconfirmed. The supervisor will review the plan.

Ask for an internal draft with visible gaps:

```text
Draft a neighbour-notice preparation note from these fictional facts.
Possible work: Practice Block, Friday requested, not approved.
Audience: supervisor reviewing a future neighbour notice.
Use a calm, plain tone. List the exact date, time, affected parties,
contact route, hazards and notification requirements as to confirm.
Do not invent a notice deadline or claim the job can proceed.
```

The responsible operator checks the product label, applicable regional plan and GROWSAFE guidance. Notice requirements vary. A message cannot establish permission to spray. Check who needs the notice and how a postponement will be communicated before sending a real version.

## Make the same check in an email

The contractor note says "hinge needs inspection; Tuesday requested; time unknown". Write "Could you confirm whether Tuesday suits?" Remove "See you at eight" unless a booking supports it. Check recipients and attachments as well as the wording.

For a vet enquiry, keep observations separate from diagnoses. Describe what was noticed and ask what information the vet needs. Seek the vet directly for an urgent concern under the farm's arrangements.

In the activity, build the prompt from supplied chips. Watch what disappears from the draft when the date or hazards are missing. Nothing is sent.
