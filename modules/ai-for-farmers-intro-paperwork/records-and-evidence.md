# Keep records and compliance drafts honest

Allow 3 minutes. All Example Station details are invented.

AI can organise evidence you supply into a table or a follow-up list. Keep the original record so someone can verify the result.

These fictional Example Station cards are teaching inputs:

| Card | Original status |
| --- | --- |
| Maintenance task | Planned; owner unassigned |
| Movement record | Official confirmation not checked |
| Equipment activity | Recorded; required inspection evidence absent |

```text
Make a draft evidence index from these cards.
Columns: item, source status, missing evidence, person to ask.
Preserve planned and unverified statuses. Do not claim compliance.
```

"Maintenance completed" would contradict the source. "Movement recorded" would turn a gap into a claim. A useful table asks the supervisor for completion evidence and the responsible record keeper for official confirmation.

## A procedure needs its conditions

The fictional visitor note says: "Sign in at the office. Wait for the supervisor before entering work areas. Report a noticed hazard." It supplies no emergency meeting place. A checklist must keep the waiting condition and ask the supervisor for missing emergency arrangements.

For real work, check the original rule or approved procedure, its date, definitions and exceptions. An adviser or responsible person checks which requirements apply. Equipment activity, completed checks and legal authorisation are different evidence. An AI summary cannot certify compliance.

Keep the checked draft separate from the source. Put unresolved questions beside it so the next person knows what still needs attention.
